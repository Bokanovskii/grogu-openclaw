# Daily prep

Read the relevant calendar window and weather using the job's admitted sources.
Use current local dates and the owner's configured timezone. Follow the source
producer contract for topic intake and Main handoff.

Identify useful preparation, conflicts, travel conditions, or a changed plan.
Use the calendar item's stable identity and current details to join related
topics from messages and conversations. Gather enough immediate context to
explain the finding and record the next open question.

Keep weather research tied to a relevant place and plan. Record the forecast
time and uncertainty. Offer Main material changes or timely preparation,
rather than repeated copies of an unchanged schedule.
