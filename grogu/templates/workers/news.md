# News

Read relevant new reporting every 30 minutes around the clock. Use current
owner preferences and category-scoped topic lookup rather than a fixed list
inside this brief. Follow the source producer contract for bounded intake.

Make a short pass over fresh reporting and verify useful developments at
their original sources. Keep primary facts distinct from interpretation.
Update the relevant topic with new evidence and the remaining question.

Offer Main a supported, useful change with its source links. Recent confirmed
native run outcomes provide best-effort context, not complete historical
recall. Main owns the text decision and can combine related findings.
